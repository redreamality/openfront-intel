## Start on the official site

**Open [openfront.io](https://openfront.io/) in your phone’s browser to play. You do not need to download an APK for the web game.**

On iPhone or iPad, use Safari’s Share menu and choose **Add to Home Screen**. Launch the new icon to play without the browser’s address bar. This is a shortcut to the web game; it does not install a separate App Store game.

On Android, you can keep playing in the browser tab. If the browser offers **Install** or **Add to Home Screen**, use that option for a convenient launcher. The label and availability depend on your browser.

For a computer, the official homepage also links to [OpenFront on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3560670/OpenFront/). That is a desktop option, not an Android or iPhone installer. Browser play and the Steam listing are different entry routes; check the store’s requirements before choosing the desktop edition.

Start with a practice match before joining a crowded public lobby. Follow the [touch controls guide](/guides/mobile-controls/) for taps, panning, pinch zoom and the action menu, or the [first-match tutorial](/guides/first-match/) for your opening decisions.

**Looking for an APK?** The browser route works without one. A similar name on a download site does not establish an official connection; follow platform links from the official game.
