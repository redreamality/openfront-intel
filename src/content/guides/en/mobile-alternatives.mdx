## Direct answer

**If you want a store-listed Android strategy game instead of OpenFront's browser route, [Territorial.io](https://territorial.io/) and [War.app](https://war.app/) are two verifiable alternatives, but neither is the same game.**

The comparison is about distribution and pacing, not a claim that one product copies another. OpenFront's official [README](https://github.com/openfrontio/OpenFrontIO/blob/v0.33.14/README.md) describes an online real-time territorial strategy game that runs in a modern browser. Territorial.io's official site exposes a multiplayer conquest game and links its [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=territorial.io). War.app's official page describes a customizable world-conquest strategy game, labels it turn-based, and promotes its [Google Play client](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.warlight).

| Choice | Verified delivery | Verified pace or focus | Best fit when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [OpenFront](https://openfront.io/) | Modern web browser | Real-time territorial control, structures, and alliances | You want the actual OpenFront rules and live matches |
| [Territorial.io](https://territorial.io/) | Browser plus an official Google Play listing | Multiplayer conquest and territorial expansion | You want another compact territory game with an Android store route |
| [War.app](https://war.app/) | Browser plus an official Google Play listing | Customizable, turn-based world conquest | You prefer taking turns over continuous real-time input |

Store availability, prices, account systems, ads, and device support can change by country and release. Follow the official product page before installing; do not treat a third-party APK mirror as equivalent to the linked store listing.

## Choose by the constraint you actually have

If the problem is only that OpenFront has no current APK, first try its [official browser route and Home Screen option](/guides/mobile-app-download/). That preserves OpenFront's live rules, maps, structures, naval units, and community. If the problem is touch execution, switching games may be unnecessary; the [mobile controls guide](/guides/mobile-controls/) explains the released gestures and recovery steps.

Choose Territorial.io when your requirement is a verified Android store listing and you still want a fast territorial-conquest interface. Choose War.app when asynchronous or turn-based planning is the important difference. Neither alternative should be presented as a drop-in replacement for OpenFront accounts, lobbies, maps, economy, nuclear rules, or controls. Start with the constraint, follow the official link, and judge the actual current product page before installing.
