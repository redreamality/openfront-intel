> Everything below is the **default** layout. Since v24 every key can be remapped in **Settings → Keybindings**, and a few can be unbound entirely. On macOS, read `Ctrl` as `Cmd`.

## "How is my opponent nuking me so fast?"

This is the question almost every new player eventually asks. There is **no external tool or autoclicker required** — it is two built-in mechanics:

1. **Build-number keys arm a structure instantly.** Press `8` and your cursor is already holding an Atom Bomb — no radial menu, no clicking through tiers. Left-click a target to launch.
2. **Nukes stay armed after firing.** When you place an Atom Bomb (`8`) or Hydrogen Bomb (`9`), the build "ghost" stays on your cursor, so you just keep left-clicking to drop bomb after bomb on different targets. Press `Esc` or right-click to stop.

So the "hold Shift and click to repeat" you remember from other RTS games is not the exact binding here — but for nukes the equivalent is even simpler: **tap `8` once, then click, click, click.** Every other building clears the ghost after one placement, so you re-tap the number for each one.

## Build & unit hotkeys

Press the number, then left-click where you want it. This skips the radial menu completely and is how fast players spam buildings (and bombs) in the late game.

| Key | Builds |
| --- | --- |
| `1` | City |
| `2` | Factory |
| `3` | Port |
| `4` | Defense Post |
| `5` | Missile Silo |
| `6` | SAM Launcher |
| `7` | Warship |
| `8` | Atom Bomb — **stays armed for repeat fire** |
| `9` | Hydrogen Bomb — **stays armed for repeat fire** |
| `0` | MIRV |

- `Enter` — build the armed unit under the cursor (instead of clicking).
- `Esc` — cancel the build preview.
- `U` — flip the launch arc direction (up / down) of an armed Atom or Hydrogen bomb.

## Mouse

Default scheme. You can flip the buttons in **Settings → "Left click opens menu"**.

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Left-click enemy / unowned border | Attack, or auto-send a transport across water |
| Left-click your land with a build armed | Place that building |
| Right-click | Open the Radial / build menu |
| `Ctrl` + left-click | Open the Build menu at that spot |
| `Alt` + left-click | Open the emote menu |
| `Shift` + drag | Box-select multiple warships |
| Drag the Attack Ratio slider | Adjust the share of Troops sent per attack |
| Mouse wheel | Zoom |
| Middle-click drag | Pan the view |

## Radial menu (reworked in v24)

Right-click your own land to open it. It is now multi-level: the core buildings sit on the ring, with SAM, Factory, warship, and upgrade options on the secondary level.

```
       Build City
            |
    Port -- ⊕ -- Defense
            |
       Missile Silo
```

The radial menu is great while you learn the icons — but once you know what you want, the **build-number keys above are far faster**.

## Action combos

- **Nuke salvo**: `8` → click each target in turn (the Atom Bomb stays armed). Switch to `9` for hydrogen bombs.
- **Coastal defense**: `3` (Port) → place it, then `4` (Defense Post) → tuck it tight against the inside of the Port.
- **Snap economy**: `1` (City) → click open ground; the ghost clears, so tap `1` again for the next one.
- **Alliance**: hover the rival and press `K`, or click their avatar → Request Alliance.

## Combat rhythm

Skilled players' mouse work rotates through three areas:

1. **The middle of your own land** — building and upgrading (mostly number keys now).
2. **The border** — attacks and transports.
3. **The minimap / player list** — watching rivals' expansion curves.

Whatever your play style, the core rule is <strong>don't leave the battlefield view</strong> — OpenFront moves fast, and 10 seconds away can be enough for a City to change hands.
