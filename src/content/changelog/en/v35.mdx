## Direct answer: what changed in OpenFront v35.0 through v35.4?

**OpenFront v35.4 fixes most public games since v0.35.3 not awarding XP, stats or rewards.** The [official v0.35.4 Release](https://github.com/openfrontio/OpenFrontIO/releases/tag/v0.35.4), published on October 24, 2026, explains that the server stopped trying to save large games too early, which could leave different levels showing on different devices. It does not promise retroactive rewards for affected games. The earlier v35 features remain smarter Nations, faster early trade, ranked FFA and Clan Wars, progression, new controls, and new maps.

Nations now save later, keep building, fortify borders before attacking, defend against landings, and respond more effectively to attacks. Hard and Impossible Nations send 1% beachhead boats followed by land attacks. They route boats around Warships, send squads at blockades, and have boat-route limits of 300 and 500 tiles respectively. In FFA, Nations can pressure a runaway leader. Easy Nations retain their old behaviour. In v0.35.3, Tribes break their alliance with a neighbouring traitor and attack that neighbour; Hard and Impossible Nations stop refusing every renewal in small games. Renewal remains a decision, not a guaranteed acceptance.

The early economy changes in v0.35.0: players with only a few Ports or Factories generate Trade Ships and Trains faster. The boost fades out by six building levels. The Release gives roughly eight seconds instead of twelve for a one-Port player, as an illustration rather than a guaranteed delivery interval. More buildings still mean more trade overall. “Stop Trading with Enemies” keeps trade with allies, and Trains can earn Gold at a station built on their existing track. These changes affect generation and legal settlement; they do not make a blocked route profitable.

Ranked FFA matches four to ten players by ELO, uses a twenty-minute timer, and ends when one human remains. Ranked Clan Wars supports two to five players per side and up to four clans, with rating assigned to the clan. Ranked requires a trusted account. Player levels add XP, a reward track, and Prestige at level 100, progressing through P1 to P10 and then Legend. Level Plutonium remains held until the account is trusted. Subscriber XP boosts differ by subscription; the progression display does not imply an in-match combat bonus.

Controls change together: R opens quick chat, F opens the emoji menu, and select-all-warships moves to X. Nukes can again hand a severed territory piece to a surrounding enemy. Public-lobby size comes from map land instead of a universal 125-player cap. New maps include Madagascar, Horn of Africa, Vancouver Island, Cape of Good Hope, Canary Islands, Bab el-Mandeb Strait, Rio de Janeiro, Pulicat Lake, Benelux, Brain Rot, and Ommatrypta.

Replay seeking is introduced in v0.35.0 but initially disabled by default for gradual rollout. v0.35.3 allows viewing while a game is still being processed; that does not guarantee identical availability on every account. That patch also improves provisional XP, adds Map Layers visibility controls, and repairs mobile attack-slider placement. These are separate changes with different consequences for the next match.

## What should you change in the next match?

If XP, statistics or rewards were missing after a public game on v0.35.3, distinguish the saving failure addressed in v0.35.4 from an ordinary provisional XP display. A level that differs between devices can be a symptom of that failure. The fix is not an XP multiplier, a new reward rate, or confirmation that past missing rewards will be restored.

Before expanding beside a Nation, inspect both the land contact and the coast. A small boat can establish a beachhead for a later land attack, while a naval blockade can attract a squad response. Keep enough reserve to defend a landing and a border at the same time. Against Hard or Impossible Nations, a route that looked safe under earlier behaviour deserves another look. Use the [Nation beachhead guide](/guides/nation-beachhead-doctrine/) for that specific decision. A legal transport route answers whether a boat can travel; it does not answer whether the landing survives.

Treat alliance renewal and betrayal as different choices. A Nation may now renew in a small game where it previously always refused, but an offer is not insurance. Keep a fallback border until acceptance is confirmed. Betraying a neighbour can also change the response of allied Tribes in v0.35.3. Check which AI neighbours touch your territory before spending the reserve elsewhere. The [AI alliance guide](/guides/v035-ai-alliance-behavior/) handles that narrower question without promising that every renewal succeeds or every AI responds identically.

Build an economy around completed journeys. The early building boost makes the first working Port or Factory more useful, but a Factory still needs a usable rail network and paying stops. Protect the destination and watch actual income before buying more capacity. For the basic question, read [what Factories do](/guides/what-do-factories-do/). Before using the global trade button, inspect neutral partners as well as enemies: keeping allied trade does not mean every non-hostile partner is spared. The [embargo answer](/guides/embargo-timing-and-trade-control/) distinguishes a targeted restriction from the global action.

Relearn the changed controls before a critical move. Pressing F from old muscle memory opens emoji instead of selecting the fleet; X is the new default fleet key. R opens quick chat for the player under the cursor. Check personal bindings in Settings before relying on those defaults. In v0.35.3, Settings > Graphics > Map Layers hides decoration across maps, and holding Space for the alternate view also hides land map layers. On Brain Rot or Ommatrypta, inspect the revealed terrain before committing to a crossing. A visibility setting changes what you can read, not the terrain itself.

Read the lobby and queue before joining. The Box can fit 210 players, Alps 185, and Middle East 170 under the map-driven system described in the Release; those examples do not promise the same count for every mode or lobby. Ranked FFA has its own smaller population and twenty-minute timer. Clan Wars has clan-sized sides and clan rating. A trusted-account lock and a daily quota are different obstacles, so use the [trusted-account answer](/guides/trusted-accounts/) rather than repeatedly retrying a locked queue.

After a nuclear strike cuts territory, inspect the new ownership before sending follow-up troops. The restored annexation behaviour can change the border and the beneficiary. For the exact capture conditions, use the [nuke-cut answer](/guides/nuke-cut-territory-annexation/). The practical checklist is to confirm the border, reserve, route, current control binding, and queue rules before spending. These checks turn the v35 changes into choices you can actually make.

## Source status, patch boundaries, and next reading

The [v0.35.4 Release](https://github.com/openfrontio/OpenFrontIO/releases/tag/v0.35.4) is this page's latest formal boundary, published on October 24, 2026. Its new item fixes public-game saving that failed too early for large games, leaving most public games since v0.35.3 without XP, stats or rewards. The note explains cross-device level differences but does not announce historical backfill or a change to XP rates.

The feature baseline is the [official v0.35.0 Release](https://github.com/openfrontio/OpenFrontIO/releases/tag/v0.35.0), published on October 16, 2026. Its overview groups Nations, progression, ranked queues, replay seeking, controls, economic generation, map-driven lobbies, and new maps. The later Release pages repeat that baseline underneath their own patch headings. Read the heading above an item before assigning its introduction date. A feature repeated in the v0.35.3 body did not necessarily first ship with v0.35.3. Earlier v34 history belongs in the [v34 notes](/changelog/v34/).

The [v0.35.1 Release](https://github.com/openfrontio/OpenFrontIO/releases/tag/v0.35.1), published on October 17 UTC, changes profile cards to use the clan tag from the latest game, adds Antarctic Tribe themes on Antarctica and Deglaciated Antarctica, and hides store, Steam promotions, and profile-share links on game portals. These are presentation and platform boundaries. They do not replace the v35 Nation strategy, ranked format, or economic generation rules. A player on a portal may therefore see a different store or sharing surface without playing a different combat system.

The [v0.35.2 Release](https://github.com/openfrontio/OpenFrontIO/releases/tag/v0.35.2), also published on October 17 UTC, restores a build preview that follows the cursor. The tile-snapping behaviour becomes the optional Snap Build Preview setting in Gameplay, off by default. It also fixes kicks failing to remove players who rejoined from another tab or device. If a placement preview looks different from an early v35 recording, inspect that setting before assuming the building rule changed. The Release body labels the patch October 16; the publication dates here use the API's UTC timestamps.

The [v0.35.3 Release](https://github.com/openfrontio/OpenFrontIO/releases/tag/v0.35.3), published on October 23, precedes the saving fix. Besides the alliance and map-visibility changes, it fixes an advertisement covering in-game controls, allows Steam purchase approval in a browser when the desktop overlay cannot appear, and adds ten quick-chat phrases. Survivors see provisional XP immediately when the game ends. Replays can be watched while processing continues. Profile cards show unverified names with their numeric suffix. A mobile attack-ratio slider placement issue and the server-side twenty-character username limit are also addressed.

The same patch repairs a water-nuke crash when a Port or Warship has no adjacent water and fixes a troop-duplication exploit. For ordinary play, the useful consequence is improved reliability; no reproduction steps are needed. Brain Rot receives more crossings and a lower central peak, impassable regions become clearer, Ommatrypta's water improves, and both Halloween maps prefer higher team counts. Read the actual map and lobby configuration rather than carrying an old screenshot's route into a new match.

Replay and XP have distinct completion states. A provisional XP figure can appear before final reconciliation, and an introduced replay feature can still be subject to rollout. Use the [replay review guide](/guides/replay-review/) when the viewer is available. Follow the focused answers linked above for detailed calculations and conditions, and return to this stable version page when a later formal patch changes the decisions.
