## Fact base (for the guide, all verified)

- Free vs paid boundary: the web game at openfront.io is and stays free ("FREE @ openfront.io," "still free in the browser, and always will be"); the Steam "STEAM EDITION" native client is a one-time paid app (US $6.99 regular, $4.96 discounted at capture, `is_free_app` false; CNY ¥29.00/¥20.59 reference). The store genre "Free to Play" describes the web game, not the client.
- What the client buys: offline/local play, Steam achievements, a full sound and music rework, better performance (assets on disk), instant trusted account status, a free month of premium (a subscription month, not permanent), and cosmetic-only Steam-exclusive skins ("never pay-to-win").
- Trusted lobbies: trusted-only public lobbies admit only accounts the API reports trusted at join; anonymous web joins are never trusted; Steam accounts skip the trust ladder and are trusted immediately on link. In v0.34.10, ~1 in 7 public games is trusted-only (`TRUSTED_PUBLIC_EVERY = 7`) and trusted lobbies cap at 25 players (`TRUSTED_MAX_PLAYER_COUNT = 25`). The free route to trusted is enough games played plus account age (thresholds deliberately unpublished, tuned against lobby fill rates); the only immediate path is a Steam link.
- Steam link mechanics: browser handoff carries a short-lived single-use ticket with a 10-minute TTL (`PENDING_LINK_TTL_MS = 10*60*1000`); a stale link errors and must be restarted; if the handoff fails (odd default browser / Linux / Steam overlay) an 8-character fallback code is shown, drawn from `23456789ABCDEFGHJKMNPQRSTVWXYZ`, and a code containing an excluded character is malformed, not corrected. Linking merges/imports the existing account (stats, cosmetics, active subscription carry over both directions; v0.34.4 merge option) rather than creating a second account; a merge throttle surfaces a wait, not a hard refusal.
- Outages/offline/recovery: v0.34.4 handles Steam outages gracefully; v0.34.8 shows offline instead of a spinner, makes session Retry visible, hides a Log-out-that-reauthenticates, and stops the stale-client lobby loop with a staged sign-in update; v0.34.9 opens lobby links in the Steam app and auto-recovers the lobby feed with a Retry button and turns away post-start joins (5s grace, `LATE_JOIN_GRACE_MS = 5_000`); v0.34.10 hands lobby links off to the production Steam app.
- Correction captured: the launch notes / official post described trusted cadence as "every 4th" / "1 in 6" at various points; the v0.34.10 tagged source value is `TRUSTED_PUBLIC_EVERY = 7`, which is the current authoritative cadence, and the team tunes the ratio.

## Version boundary

Applies to v0.34.0 (Steam launch) through v0.34.10 (current, 17 Sep 2026). Pre-v0.34.0 builds have no Steam client, no trusted-only public lobbies, no Steam link, and no Steam-outage/offline/lobby-link recovery paths described here. Post-v0.34.10 trusted cadence and thresholds may change (the team tunes them); re-verify the `TRUSTED_PUBLIC_EVERY` and threshold values in the tagged source if the cadence matters for a future update.
